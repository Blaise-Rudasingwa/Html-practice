Vegetable Omelette Recipe

This vegetable omelette recipe is easy to make and healthy. It can be taken as breakfast, lunch, and light dinner as well.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • 1 small tomato (chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon chopped green pepper
  • A pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil or butter

Instructions

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl
  2. Add the salt and black pepper
  3. Beat the eggs until well mixed
  4. Stir in the chopped onion,tomato, and green pepper
  5. Heat the oil or butter in a frying pan
  6. Pour the egg mixture into the pan
  7. Cook for about 2-3 minutes on each side
  8. Carefully flip the omelette and cook the other side for another 2 minutes
  9. Serve while still hot

Optional Toppings

  • Grated cheese
  • Fresh parsley
  • Avocado slices
  • Tomato ketchup
